Main flow: Dollars → Soles

Run with $100 at the Compra 3.420 rate. Origin bank BCP, destination “Sueldo Soles” (Scotiabank), source of funds “Ahorros”. Those picks were arbitrary; they only exist to reach the next screen.

0

Inicio: the calculator

Entry point and default tab. The user chooses the direction, types an amount and sees the converted amount live.

User does

  • Picks Compra 3.420 (they send USD) or Venta 3.460 (they send PEN)
  • Types “¿Cuánto envías?”; “Entonces recibes” updates (100 USD → 342.00 PEN)
  • Optionally swaps currencies with the circular arrows
  • Optionally enters a coupon and taps APLICAR
  • Taps INICIAR OPERACIÓN

Also on screen

  • Koinks counter beside the calculator (150 for this amount, so it looks like the reward for this operation, not the balance)
  • “¿Monto mayor a $5.000 o S/18.000? Click para Tipo de Cambio Preferencial”
  • Floating WhatsApp button (dismissible) and a scroll-to-top button
  • Bottom tabs: Inicio · Historial · Cuentas · Koinks · Perfil
1

Datos: “Completa los datos”

wizard 1 / 3

After INICIAR OPERACIÓN a brand logo loading overlay appears for ~2–3 s, then the form. Three required selections gate the CONTINUAR button (pale until all are set, teal when complete).

Summary card (read-only)

  • Tú envías $ 100.00 · Tú recibes S/ 342.00
  • Koinks 150 · Tipo de cambio utilizado 3.420
  • Blue banner: “recibirás tu dinero en 15 minutos aproximadamente”

Fields

  • ¿Desde qué banco nos envías tu dinero? → bank list
  • ¿En qué cuenta deseas recibir tu dinero? → saved accounts for the receive currency
  • Origen de fondos → compliance declaration
Rule shown inline: “ambas cuentas (origen y destino) deben estar a tu nombre. Kambista no transfiere a cuentas de terceros.”
2

Transferencia: “Transfiere a Kambista”

wizard 2 / 3

CONTINUAR registers the operation and shows where to send the money. The user leaves the app, makes a bank transfer to Kambista, then comes back.

Instructions shown

  • 1. Transfiere 100.00 dólares desde tu banco BCP a nuestra cuenta bancaria.
  • 2. Guarda el número de operación para el siguiente paso.
  • Timer line: “El tipo de cambio podría actualizarse a las: 17:18”

Kambista account card

  • Titular: Kambista SAC
  • Monto a transferir (copy icon)
  • Banco: BCP (matches the user’s origin bank in this run)
  • Tipo de cuenta: Corriente · Dólares, with a warning to use the DÓLARES account
  • Número de cuenta (copy) · RUC Kambista (copy)
  • “Kambista NO realiza cobros automáticos de tus cuentas.”
Out-of-hours behaviour: captured on a Saturday at 17:03. A dismissible toast said “¡Te encuentras fuera de horario! Tu operación se verificará el lunes, 12/10/2026 a las 09:00 am”.

Constancia

not entered

The third wizard step is labelled “Constancia”, reached from YA HICE MI OPERACIÓN. It was deliberately not opened: that button tells Kambista a real bank transfer was made, which was not the case. See Not captured.

Sell direction: Soles → Dollars

Same calculator, other rate tab. Only the tab and currencies flip; the wizard after it is the same shape (not walked through).

100 ÷ 3.460 = 28.90, so Venta divides and Compra multiplies. The Historial examples below were sell-direction (Enviaste S/ …, Recibiste $ …).

After the operation: Historial

Where a user tracks an exchange and gets receipts.

Operation lifecycle (from a finished operation)

  • Operación registrada 15:20 · user completed the wizard
  • Verificamos tu transferencia 15:42 · Kambista confirmed the incoming deposit
  • Enviamos tu dinero 15:43 · payout to the user’s account
  • Recibiste tu dinero 15:43 · done, 23 minutes end-to-end

Other tabs

Supporting screens a user touches around the exchange.

Koinks connect back to the flow: the calculator shows the Koinks tied to the amount, and the home banner “¡Gana CUPONES! Canjea tus Koinks y ahorra” points to redeeming them. Inferred, not tested: redeemed coupons are used in the calculator’s coupon field.

Not captured (to explore next)

Left out on purpose or not reached. Each would need either a real transfer or a side effect on the account.

Skipped to avoid side effects

  • Constancia step and what YA HICE MI OPERACIÓN asks for (likely an operation number or voucher; unverified)
  • “Agregar cuenta” form and edit/delete account
  • Applying a coupon, “Tipo de Cambio Preferencial”, CANJEAR on Koinks

Not opened

  • “¿Cuándo recibo mi dinero?” chip
  • Datos personales, Refiere y gana, Alertas de tipo de cambio, Cambiar perfil
  • Login / registration / identity verification (the device was already signed in)

Not triggered

  • Validation and error states (empty amount, over the limit, minimum amount)
  • Behaviour inside business hours (the capture was on a Saturday evening)
  • A Venta (soles → dollars) run through the wizard
  • An operation sitting in “En proceso”
